Designing & Evaluating a Graduate Preview Weekend

A weekend program for prospective Ph.D. applicants to Duke Psychology & Neuroscience, built from scratch, run on a $10K budget, and evaluated with a pre/post survey.

Program

Prospective graduate student program, 2023

Participants

89 applicants → 13 attendees

Evaluation

Pre/post survey, 12 of 13 responded (92%)

Headline finding

Attendees’ ratings rose on all 12 survey items from before to after the program, with the largest gains in sense of belonging. Themes from the open-ended responses guided improvements for future years.

Challenge Methodology & Scope Insights
01Challenge

Applicants without insider access apply at a disadvantage

Ph.D. admissions reward unwritten knowledge, like which faculty are taking students and what a strong statement sounds like. Applicants without mentors in academia often learn it late or not at all. With no program in place, a graduate-student team built one: a selective weekend of faculty meetings, one-on-one application feedback and a writing workshop, held before applications were due.

Evaluation

Attendees rated 12 statements on a 1–5 agreement scale before and after the weekend, then rated satisfaction and answered open-ended questions on strengths and weaknesses. 12 of 13 responded to both the pre and post surveys.

03Insights & recommendations

What the evaluation showed, and what changed

1Insight

Gains were largest where applicants had the least access before

Every item rose, but not evenly. Belonging items gained about 1.2 points on average and application readiness about 1.0. Research fit and intent to apply gained least, because attendees already rated them high.

Recommendation

Protect the time attendees spend directly with current students and faculty. Those sessions target the biggest gaps, and attendees' top request was for more of them.

2Insight

Open-ended feedback guided improvements

Satisfaction was high (4.83 of 5 overall), so the written feedback did more to guide changes than the scores.

My role

One of three organizing leads and co-head of the logistics team. I co-designed the program, ran logistics for each attendee's visit, and led budget planning and management. I helped write the survey and interpret the results; other team members ran the analyses.
