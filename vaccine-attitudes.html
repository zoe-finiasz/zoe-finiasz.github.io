Case Study — Applied Behavioral Research

Vaccine Attitudes & Institutional Trust

A national survey study examining how conspiracy‑belief endorsement shapes trust in evidence — and whether that distrust is general or targeted specifically at institutional sources.

Sample
801 U.S. adults, Prolific
Method
Survey + attitude scaling
Analysis
R — cleaning, modeling, aggregation
Role
Design, analysis & visualization
Headline finding

Conspiracy endorsers don't distrust evidence in general — they selectively withhold trust from institutional sources of evidence specifically, while remaining receptive to evidence from other channels.
