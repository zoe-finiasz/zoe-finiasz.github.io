Zoe Finiasz, Ph.D.

I am a behavioral research scientist who studies how people reason and make decisions. I use mixed-methods research to explore how we learn from museum exhibits, public health messaging, and others around us.

I'm particularly interested in how children and adults decide who and what to trust when they are learning. My approach combines qualitative & quantitative methodology with mixed-effects modeling to understand beliefs and behaviors.
