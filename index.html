Zoe Finiasz, Ph.D.

I’m a behavioral scientist who studies how people learn what they believe. Small details, like how a sentence is phrased or who is saying it, can change what people understand. My work looks for the details that make the biggest difference for learners. I also test whether programs designed to support learners actually work.
